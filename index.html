Room to spread out, quiet enough to hear the water

Lawn to the water's edge

Wide green lawns run down to the dam, with shaded paths under mature trees. Hold the ceremony on the waterfront and the reception under the trees.

Set back from the highway

The garden is just off Thika Superhighway at Exit 13, but the grounds sit away from the road, so the noise stays behind you.

Space for the whole guest list

From a small birthday to about 600 guests, the same lawn works. Move the guest slider above and see how the garden fills.

What the garden is used for

Wedding receptions. Graduation parties. Corporate team building. Church workshops. Photoshoots. Picnics by the water. Quiet dates at the end of the day.
